# Privacy Policy for PJ's Baby Tracker

Last updated: March 2026

## Overview
PJ's Baby Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. We collect as little data as possible and never sell your information.

## Data We Collect
PJ's Baby Tracker stores the following data:
- Baby activity logs (feeding times, sleep, notes)
- Custom activity preferences
- Baby name (optional, stored locally)

## Where Your Data Lives
- All data is stored locally on your device by default
- If you choose to use Family Sync, your activity data is stored in a private Firebase database accessible only via your unique 6-character family code
- We do not have access to your family code or your data

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information
- We do not track your location
- We do not use advertising or analytics
- We do not sell any data to third parties

## In-App Purchases
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not store or have access to any payment information.

## Children's Privacy
This app is designed for use by parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Firebase
If Family Sync is enabled, data is stored using Google Firebase. You can review Google's privacy policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
